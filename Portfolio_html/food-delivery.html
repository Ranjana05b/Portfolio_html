How It Works

From Order to Delivery—Fully Orchestrated

A clear operational workflow that keeps customers, vendors, riders, and admins in sync.

1

Customer Places Order

Customer browses restaurants, customizes items, applies offers, and checks out via app or website.

2

Vendor Accepts

Restaurant receives the order instantly, confirms availability, and starts preparation.

3

Delivery Assigned

A nearby rider is assigned automatically or manually based on your dispatch rules.

4

Live Tracking

Customer and admin monitor pickup and drop-off with real-time location updates.

5

Order Delivered

Rider completes delivery with confirmation; customer can rate the experience.

6

Admin Monitoring

Operations team oversees SLAs, settlements, disputes, and performance analytics.
